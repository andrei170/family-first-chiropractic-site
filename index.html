Our Practice

A practice built around preconception, pregnancy, and the early years.

Family First Chiropractic is a two-practitioner family-wellness practice in Northeast Wisconsin. Dr. Jodi Rabas, DC and Dr. Joe Huss, DC see patients at 443 S. Jackson Street in Green Bay three days a week, and at 305 Steele Street in Algoma every Tuesday.

The specialty is the practice. Preconception care for couples preparing for pregnancy. Prenatal chiropractic across the full pregnancy window. Webster Technique territory. Postpartum care. Pediatric chiropractic from the newborn period through childhood-development stages. The work spans the family lifecycle, not just the single-visit patient.

"The mother who chooses this practice for pregnancy often brings the newborn, then the toddler, then the school-age child. That is the relationship Family First Chiropractic is built to hold."

The Algoma clinic is not a vanity address. It is a real weekly practice day in a Kewaunee County community where very few family chiropractors have a presence. Two counties, one practice, one tightly defined specialty.

The Specialty

Prenatal, pediatric, and family chiropractic care from preconception to childhood.

It is rare for a chiropractic practice to commit this fully to a single named specialty. Family First Chiropractic is not a general chiropractor that sometimes sees pregnant mothers. The specialty is the entire shape of the practice.

  • Preconception care for couples preparing for pregnancy
  • Prenatal chiropractic across the pregnancy window, in Webster Technique territory
  • Webster Technique, a specific named prenatal chiropractic approach
  • Postpartum care for mothers after birth
  • Newborn and infant chiropractic from the earliest weeks of life
  • Pediatric chiropractic across childhood-development stages
  • Family wellness for the whole household over the long term
"When a chiropractic practice is built for the family lifecycle, a first visit during pregnancy becomes care that continues across children and years."

Frequently Asked

Questions families ask before booking.

Six questions the practice hears from new prenatal and pediatric patients. If yours is not here, call (920) 940-8711.

Who are the practitioners at Family First Chiropractic?

Family First Chiropractic is a two-practitioner family-wellness practice. Dr. Jodi Rabas, DC and Dr. Joe Huss, DC both provide prenatal, preconception, pediatric, postpartum, and family chiropractic care across the Green Bay and Algoma clinics. Specific credentialing details are confirmed at intake.

What does Family First Chiropractic specialize in?

The practice is anchored to preconception, prenatal, postpartum, pediatric, and birth-process and childhood-development care. The work spans the family lifecycle, from couples preparing for pregnancy through the prenatal window, into postpartum and infant care, and across childhood-development stages.

How many clinics does the practice have?

Two. The main practice is at 443 S. Jackson Street in Green Bay, open three days a week. The Algoma clinic is at 305 Steele Street and is open every Tuesday. Both clinics are part of the same practice, with the same practitioners and the same specialty focus.

Do you see patients during every trimester of pregnancy?

Yes. The practice works with prenatal patients across the pregnancy window, including during all three trimesters. The first visit includes a history review and an exam so that care is appropriate for where you are in your pregnancy. Call (920) 940-8711 to book and confirm which clinic works best for your schedule.

When can a baby first be seen for chiropractic care?

The practice sees newborn and infant patients from the early weeks of life as part of its pediatric care offering. Every pediatric and infant reference at Family First Chiropractic is written in generic, non-outcome-promise form, and care is appropriate to the age and stage of the child. Call to discuss your specific situation.

How do I become a new patient?

Call the practice at (920) 940-8711. The team will help you pick the right clinic and the right day, whether that is the Green Bay clinic on South Jackson Street or the Algoma clinic on Tuesdays. The first visit covers your history, a thorough exam, and a plan that fits your family.
